Hajj packing list

The whole pilgrimage, step by step — gender-correct ihram.

79+ things Get this list in Zād — our dedicated Umrah & Hajj app, offline

Before you leave

  • Passport
  • Hajj/Umrah visa & permit
  • Meningitis vaccination certificate
  • Passport photos (4–6)
  • Payment card
  • Cash
  • Bum bag / money belt
  • Padlock for luggage
  • ID lanyard / group card
  • Health insurance card
  • Doctor's letter (English)
  • Hotel card in Arabic

Ihram – men

  • Ihram garments
  • Safety pins
  • Ihram belt (money belt)

Ihram – women

  • Abaya / jilbab ×2–3
  • Hijabs / scarves (women)
  • Tampons / pads
  • Period-delay pills
  • Under-scarves
  • Hijab pins & magnets
  • Prayer garment

Ihram & worship

  • Prayer mat
  • Qur'an / dua book
  • Tasbih (prayer beads)
  • Miswak (siwak)
  • Spray bottle / hand fan

Mina (tent camp)

  • Sleeping mat / sheet
  • Light travel blanket
  • Unscented wet wipes
  • Face masks
  • Earplugs

Arafat & Muzdalifah

  • Sunscreen
  • Electrolyte sachets
  • Snacks & glucose
  • Pebble bag (Jamarat)

Tawaf & sa'i

  • Sandals
  • Bathroom flip-flops
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Umbrella
  • Sunglasses
  • Cooling towel
  • Shoe bag (for tawaf)

Toiletries

  • No perfume during ihram
  • Unscented soap & toiletries
  • Toothbrush & toothpaste
  • Hairbrush
  • Hair ties
  • Nail clippers & file
  • Unscented Vaseline / anti-chafing
  • Lip balm
  • Tissues
  • Contact lenses & solution
  • Glasses & case
  • Unscented deodorant

Health & medicine

  • First-aid kit
  • Regular medication & prescriptions
  • Children's paracetamol
  • Paracetamol / painkillers
  • Hand sanitiser
  • Antihistamine / allergy
  • Diarrhoea tablets & rehydration
  • Vitamins
  • Compression socks
  • Blister plasters
  • Throat lozenges

Stay & daily

  • Underwear & socks
  • Underwear & socks (child)
  • Pyjamas
  • T-shirts
  • Trousers
  • Jacket
  • Hat / cap
  • Sun hat
  • Backpack
  • Power bank
  • Chargers
  • Travel adapter
  • Travel laundry soap
