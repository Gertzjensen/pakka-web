Privacy Policy

Pakka — packing lists for every trip

Pakka collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your packing lists, your family setup and your trips stay on your device and in your own private iCloud — never on any server of ours.

Last updated: 23 August 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Pakka has no servers of its own, no user accounts, and no advertising or analytics SDKs. Your data goes only to your own device and, if you use iCloud, to your own private Apple account — never to us.

Where your data lives

Everything you create is stored on your device. The app also remembers your chosen language, on your device only.

Exactly what that is

So you can judge this for yourself rather than take our word for it, here is every kind of information Pakka stores:

  • The people you travel with — the name you give each of them, and optionally a date of birth and a gender. Both are optional; the date of birth is used only to work out whether someone counts as a baby, a child or an adult, and the gender only where a list splits by it.
  • Your trips — the title, the type of trip, the dates you are away, and the countries you travel from and to.
  • How you are travelling — transport, purpose, whether a pet or a hire car is coming, and for some trips a sport, a hunting type or a species.
  • The packing list itself — every item, its quantity, and whether you have ticked it off. This includes anything you add yourself.

Two of these deserve saying out loud. The dates tell anyone who can read them when you are not at home. And choosing Umrah or Hajj as your trip type says something about your religion, which European law treats as a special category of personal data. That is exactly why the list is kept in your own private iCloud and never on a server of ours — but you should know it is in there.

If you are signed in to iCloud, your data is also kept in your own private iCloud database, so it survives a new phone and syncs between your own devices. That database belongs to you and is managed by Apple — we have no access to it and no way to read it. It is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. If you are not signed in to iCloud, everything simply stays on the device.

To remove your data, delete the app. To also remove the iCloud copy, go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and delete Pakka’s data.

Notifications

If you turn on packing reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No reminder data leaves your phone, and no server is involved. You can turn reminders off at any time in iOS Settings.

Sharing a list

When you tap “Share list”, you choose what to share and with whom, using your phone’s standard share sheet. Once a list leaves the app that way, where it goes is up to you and the app you sent it with.

Apart from the iCloud sync described above, Pakka sends nothing anywhere — and iCloud only ever goes to your own account, never to us.

Purchases

Unlocking Pakka is handled entirely by Apple’s App Store. We never see your payment details, and we receive no personal information about you from Apple — only anonymous sales totals.

Third parties

None. No advertising networks, no analytics providers, no trackers, no data brokers.

Children

Pakka is suitable for all ages (rated 4+). Because we collect no personal data at all, there is nothing collected from children either.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. To remove everything, delete the app from your device — and, if you use iCloud, also delete Pakka’s data under iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to support@getpakka.app.
