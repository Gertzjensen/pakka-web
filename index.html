What Pakka does

A list per personBabies, children and adults each get their own items, in the right amounts for the days you are away.
Fits the tripBeach, ski, cabin, camping, festival, business, pilgrimage — 22 kinds of trip, each with its own list.
Knows the destinationPlug type and voltage, emergency numbers, currency, expected weather and the time difference.
The essentials stay on topPassport, medicine, chargers and cards are pinned above everything else, so they are never scrolled past.
Closes up the house tooWater off, bins out, plants, post, lights on a timer — the list you always forget half of.
Yours aloneNo account and no tracking. Lists sync through your own private iCloud, which means we never see them.
