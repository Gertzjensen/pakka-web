Umrah packing list

The short pilgrimage — men's and women's ihram, fragrance-free.

63+ things Get this list in Zād — our dedicated Umrah & Hajj app, offline

Before you leave

  • Passport
  • Health insurance card
  • Payment card
  • Cash
  • Doctor's letter (English)
  • Hotel card in Arabic
  • Hajj/Umrah visa & permit
  • Meningitis vaccination certificate

Ihram – men

  • Ihram garments
  • Ihram belt (money belt)
  • Safety pins

Ihram – women

  • Abaya / jilbab ×2–3
  • Under-scarves
  • Hijab pins & magnets
  • Prayer garment
  • Tampons / pads
  • Period-delay pills

Ihram & worship

  • Prayer mat
  • Qur'an / dua book
  • Tasbih (prayer beads)
  • Miswak (siwak)
  • Spray bottle / hand fan
  • Pocket Quran / app

Tawaf & sa'i

  • Comfortable walking sandals
  • Shoe bag (for tawaf)
  • Small crossbody bag
  • Cooling towel
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Umbrella
  • Sunglasses

Toiletries

  • No perfume during ihram
  • Unscented soap
  • Unscented deodorant
  • Unscented wet wipes
  • Toothbrush & toothpaste
  • Hairbrush
  • Hair ties
  • Nail clippers & file
  • Contact lenses & solution
  • Glasses & case

Health & medicine

  • Regular medication & prescriptions
  • Electrolyte sachets
  • Anti-chafing balm
  • Blister plasters
  • Hand sanitiser
  • Sunscreen
  • Insect repellent
  • Face masks
  • Saline nasal spray
  • Throat lozenges
  • First-aid kit
  • Paracetamol / painkillers
  • Children's paracetamol

Stay & daily

  • ID lanyard / group card
  • Power bank
  • Travel adapter
  • Travel laundry soap
  • Underwear & socks
  • Underwear & socks (child)
  • Pyjamas
  • Jumpers
  • Chargers
  • Sun hat
