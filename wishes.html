What happens to your wish

A person reads it.

Pakka is made by one developer. Every message is read, and the ones that come up more than once move to the top of the list.

Updates are free. All of them.

Pakka is a single one-time purchase. New trip types, new items and new features are included — there is no subscription, and there never will be. A list built because you asked for it costs you nothing extra.

We cannot promise a date.

Some wishes are an afternoon; some are months. We would rather say nothing than promise a month we cannot keep.

Found something wrong instead?

Bugs, wrong items, a bad translation, or something that reads oddly in your language — those go to the same place, and they jump the queue. Write to support@getpakka.app.
